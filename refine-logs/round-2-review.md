## What passed conceptually

- The statistical object can be the complete frozen stochastic `AgentPolicy`; its changing per-origin ASTs need no individual repeated support.
- Every planned origin, invalid output, timeout, and fallback must stay in the policy denominator and be aggregated within physical unit first.
- A single online proposer plus compiler/verifier is a substantive Agent architecture; four-role prompting is unnecessary.

## Remaining blockers

1. Seal the complete policy registry before policy-validation outcomes; multiplicity correction cannot repair adaptive prompt/grammar redesign on reused units.
2. Define `ENUM-BFS` as a strictly label-blind online policy with frozen score, expansion/tie rules, and separate development, selection, and online budgets.
3. Add `D1-RAW` and `D1-PACKET`; the latter must receive the same bytes as hybrid arms and directly emit numbers.
4. Repair transform semantics so `SHIFT` and `INFLATE` name a finite base action; use one AST per target-horizon and only syntactic normalization.
5. Keep one-call/no-retry accuracy runs separate from transport-resilience runs.
6. Run only compiler, mock, blind-replay, and synthetic-search tests until a real target Data Gate and authenticated AgentPlan capability Gate pass.

## Claim ceiling

Deterministic verification can certify contract behavior; whole-unit OOF can qualify only the complete frozen policy's risk. Neither certifies the predictive correctness of a unique AST or a causal effect. The narrow provisional novelty is the separation of those two validity layers.
