## Final Proposal Snapshot

- One paper-level thesis: determine whether complete direct, tool-grounded, and multi-call LLM architecture packages improve real held-out capacitor forecasts under strictly causal and observable matched budgets.
- Primary arms: `N0,D1-RAW,D1-PACKET,H1,RF1,RC1,ACT1,ENUM-ACTION`; `IF1` is only a representation ablation.
- Primary typed authority: 19 exact Actions around a common outer-training `N0/FALLBACK`; the 96 compositional set is appendix-only.
- Evaluation: prediction-before-reveal, sealed outer whole-unit CV, common planned keys, fallback-retaining intention-to-treat scoring, and physical-unit paired inference.
- Minimum multi-call controls: pre-sealed `D4-H` and `D4-X` with a fixed componentwise-median aggregator; interpret D4-X as a roster effect unless constituent ability is controlled.

## Method Evolution Highlights

1. **Contribution reset:** a proof killed the per-origin program-synthesis expressivity claim; the work is now an empirical architecture study rather than a renamed router method.
2. **Exact authority factorization:** the primary controller was reduced from a compositional 96-Action class to a literal 19-Action union that can be compared with model, fusion, and correction subsets.
3. **Evaluation repair:** the design chose sealed outer whole-unit CV, froze the planned-key execution/maturity state machine, and retained fallback/failure in the main denominator.
4. **Multi-Agent restraint:** homogeneous and heterogeneous four-call forecast ensembles are mandatory controls; debate, hierarchy, reflection, and dynamic routing are deferred.
5. **Target restraint:** capacitance, Ah capacity, ESR/IR, SOH, EOL, and RUL are kept separate; unsupported targets remain `NA`.

## Evidence and Novelty Boundaries

The same-family novelty check identified strong collisions with direct LLM time-series forecasting, multi-Agent forecasting, tool-augmented quantitative agents, LLM expert routing/fusion, and cheaper learned routers. The safe contribution is a controlled capacitor-domain empirical design. “First,” “novel router,” “new multi-Agent framework,” and causal counterfactual language are prohibited without independent support.

Ren and Patrizi have public metadata, licenses, payload routes, sizes, and hashes, but have not yet passed row-level audit. Benchmark-L is scientifically blocked and Stress-2 is sanity-only. No accuracy result exists at this refinement boundary.
