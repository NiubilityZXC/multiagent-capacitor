## Overall Evolution

- The project moved from a broad multi-Agent graph and pseudo-novel finite routing claim to a falsifiable empirical comparison of complete architecture packages.
- Direct raw LLM forecasting and same-expert-packet direct forecasting are mandatory controls, not contributions.
- Typed tool selection, fusion, correction/calibration, and a unified controller now share an exact 19-Action authority manifest around one common numerical fallback.
- The deterministic `ENUM-ACTION` control can falsify LLM-controller necessity; `IF1` can only measure representation/elicitation effects.
- One physical attempt, no accuracy repair, prediction-before-reveal, failure-retaining planned denominators, and physical-unit inference prevent retries, fallback, and rolling-window pseudo-replication from masquerading as accuracy.
- Sealed outer whole-unit CV replaced the contradictory “global untouched shadow plus LOCO” wording. A separately untouched corpus remains an external stress, not pooled confirmation.
- Multi-Agent complexity was cut to the minimum D4-H/D4-X four-call controls. Debate, hierarchy, reflection, and dynamic routing are optional appendices only after the core study is eligible.
