## Key findings

1. **Observation**: the online harness generated every planned context=4 prediction before revealing the next event, matured all 1,296 predictions, and recorded no model or scoring failure. **Interpretation**: the parser, nested LOCO selection, rolling event-step horizons, durable commit barrier, and unit-macro metrics are operational on Stress-2. **Implication**: Benchmark S is useful as a deterministic leakage/integrity harness. **Next step**: reuse the same ledger contract without relaxing it when the large-pack parser becomes eligible.

2. **Observation**: Ridge has the lowest observed MAE in every executed Stress-2 target-horizon-context cell, with context=4 descriptive skill of 73.2% to 89.4% for capacity and 67.9% to 89.1% for ESR versus last value. **Interpretation**: a small direct-increment model is a credible candidate for the larger benchmark. **Implication**: it deserves entry into the frozen candidate set, but not promotion: N=6 is below the confirmatory minimum, identities are column surrogates, and the same dataset informed harness development. **Next step**: evaluate the unchanged Ridge implementation under identity-safe nested LOCO on an independently auditable larger dataset.

3. **Observation**: all non-persistence candidates show positive descriptive skill versus last value in the primary run, while absolute error generally grows with horizon for the simple extrapolators. Ridge's ESR h=3 MAE is lower than its h=2 MAE, illustrating that overlapping origins and a tiny sample can yield non-monotone aggregate patterns. **Interpretation**: horizon trends from this harness are not a stable scientific estimate. **Implication**: per-unit results and event counts must accompany aggregates. **Next step**: retain unit-clustered inference and physical-time horizons after acquisition-time semantics are frozen.

4. **Observation**: the quick design simulation keeps null and harmful promotion point estimates low, but N=6 has only 17.5% to 25.5% power at a true 10% improvement, N=12/K=1 reaches 78.5%, and N=12/K=5 reaches only 52.0% correct champion. **Interpretation**: multiple candidate routing and short trajectories impose a severe power cost. **Implication**: a numerical leaderboard alone cannot justify dynamic model selection. **Next step**: run the preregistered formal simulation only after the candidate count and independent-unit inventory are frozen; require “no champion” when power and multiplicity criteria fail.

5. **Observation**: 13 QT2 repeats contained at least one unit with no mature origin and were preserved as explicit FAIL. **Interpretation**: the failure-parity path is active. **Implication**: availability failures lower planned-denominator rates instead of disappearing. **Next step**: keep these rows in every future evaluation and separately report analyzable-only estimates only as diagnostics.

## Claim boundaries

Supported now:

- The downloaded public files have the recorded hashes and the implemented Stress-2 parser/replay harness runs deterministically.
- Capacity/ESR one-to-three-event-step prediction ledgers, raw errors, and explicit failure accounting can be generated under nested LOCO with a durable causal barrier.
- The quick design simulator deterministically exercises null, beneficial, harmful, small-N, missingness, and K=5 decision cases.

Not supported now:

- Any statement that Ridge or another model is the most accurate capacitor prognostics model.
- Any RUL, SOH lifetime, uncertainty-calibration, cross-condition, physical-time-horizon, or deployment-accuracy claim.
- Any claim that Benchmark L passed the Data Gate, that 24 provisional labels are 24 independent physical capacitors, or that the large pack contains direct ESR/termination labels.
- Any Design Gate pass or multi-agent architecture advantage.

## Required next experiments

1. Freeze and test the large-pack reference-aware EIS parser: event/replicate linkage, acquisition-time ordering, 20-token-to-18-column mapping, frequency rule, and ES12 off-by-four handling.
2. Resolve or explicitly retain uncertainty for physical identity, duplicates, and termination. If outcome/termination remains unknown, keep RUL completely NA and evaluate only auditable trajectories.
3. Freeze target extraction on training units only: direct Cs/Cp at a preregistered frequency or a preregistered equivalent-circuit fit; do not label an arbitrary Re(Z) bin as ESR.
4. After the identity/time/target gate passes, run the unchanged six-model suite with nested LOCO within condition and separate leave-one-condition-out evaluation. Do not mix the estimands.
5. Re-run the full design grid at preregistered repeat counts only after the independent-unit count and candidate family are known. If power or coverage criteria fail, return `NO_CHAMPION` rather than tuning thresholds post hoc.
