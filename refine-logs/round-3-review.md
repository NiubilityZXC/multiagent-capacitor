## Decisive equivalence result

At a realized origin every predicate value is already present in the packet before synthesis. Therefore

\[
r_x(IF(q,a,b))=a\;\text{or}\;b
\]

and for every stochastic program policy there is an action-only policy with the same conditional distribution over committed forecasts. The 2,052,096 syntactic programs partition into 96 active-action fibers of 21,376 syntactic representations each. Stochastic decoding does not change this result.

A fixed AST can still be replayed under branch-flipping perturbations to test an artifact-level metamorphic contract. It does not verify the complete resynthesizing LLM policy on the perturbed packet and does not add per-origin predictive expressivity.
